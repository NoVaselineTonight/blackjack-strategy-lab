How it is calculated

Every starting hand (55 card pairs, by exact composition) is solved against every upcard by recursing through every card that can still come out of the shoe, for you and for the dealer, with cards removed as they are dealt. Hard and soft cells show the probability-weighted result over all two-card hands with that total.

Every expected value shown is for the whole hand, so it already includes the chance that the dealer has blackjack (that is why surrender against a 10 shows about −54% rather than −50% with a peeking dealer). With a peeking dealer, decisions are made knowing the dealer does not have blackjack. With no hole card, dealer blackjack is part of the outcome and takes doubled and split bets unless "original bet only" is set. Split hands use the standard approximation: each one draws from the shoe with the pair and upcard removed. Free Bet hands are valued by their real stake, so a free hand that loses costs nothing.
